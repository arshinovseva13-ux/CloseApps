# Secure Builder

## Workflow
Identify assets, trust boundaries, attacker input, and impact. Validate type, size, format, and range; encode output by context. Use parameterized queries and safe DOM APIs. Enforce authorization at a trusted boundary. Keep secrets out of source and logs. Treat uploads as hostile and prevent traversal. Add malicious-input tests and report evidence, impact, severity, and remediation. Do not add paid APIs.

## Final report
Summarize decisions, changed files, checks actually run, results, and limitations. Separate verified facts from assumptions.
